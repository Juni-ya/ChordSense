# Director's Treatment

## Director Brief
- **Visual Thesis:** A forensic, monolithic acoustic observatory suspended in fog—translating continuous audio waves into decipherable harmonic architecture without decorative noise.
- **Signature Technique 1 (Monolithic Horizon):** Widescreen letterbox framing ($2.39:1$) with top and bottom telemetry gutters housing non-intrusive metadata, leaving the central observation plane clean.
- **Signature Technique 2 (The Glass Wall):** The central playback and active chord element is treated as an acoustic specimen chamber—stark, high-contrast, framed by subtle technical corner brackets (`#8`).
- **Signature Technique 3 (Forensic Telemetry):** Clean monospaced readouts, precise numerical decibels and BPM counters, cross marks (`#10`), and solid amber phosphor indicators (`#d4a35f`).
- **Motion Rules:** Slow, deliberate spring physics (`stiffness: 280, damping: 30`). Transitions occur with quiet weight. No bouncy cartoons, no flashing rave lights, no arbitrary neon pulse.
- **Typography Rules:** Monospaced technical telemetry (`SF Mono`, `ui-monospace`) paired with a monumental, heavy San Francisco Pro headline hierarchy (`tracking-tight`, crisp solid white `#eceeed`).

## Site Cinematic Grammar
- **Page-Shell Logic:** Panoramic Slab container enclosed by letterbox viewports ($2.39:1$ anamorphic aesthetic). The shell feels like military/scientific telemetry installed inside an extraterrestrial observation deck.
- **Navigation Posture:** De-emphasized top horizon bar—a thin, translucent technical bridge (`border-b border-zinc-800 bg-[#08090a]/80 backdrop-blur-xl`) with coordinate indicators and minimalist session controls.
- **Framing Discipline:** Monolithic rectangular geometry with $0\text{px}$ to $4\text{px}$ radii. Strict vertical and horizontal alignment lines (`border border-[#2a333a]`). Corner brackets (`#8`) and hairline crosses (`#10`).
- **Density Cadence:** High negative space in the initial signal ingestion, tightening into dense, high-precision forensic telemetry during active harmonic playback.
- **Recurring Material Layers:**
  - Base: Deep obsidian void (`#08090a`)
  - Substrate: Charcoal slate (`#111417`)
  - Specimen Card: Muted steel slate (`#181e22`) with solid hairline borders
  - Highlight Accent: Incandescent amber phosphor (`#d4a35f`)
- **Allowed Composition Families:**
  - `Cutaway Monolith` (Letterbox Cinema `#4`)
  - `Center-Weighted Monument` (`#1`)
  - `Horizontal Overflow Reveal` (`#24` continuous spectrogram tape)
- **What May Repeat:** Hairline coordinate dividers, monospaced metadata stamps, technical status glyphs.
- **What Must Vary:** Each operational section (Ingest, Chamber, Timeline, Calibration) has a distinct structural posture.
- **Demo Uniqueness Guardrail:**
  - Guaranteed zero convergence with prior bento dashboard. No centered SaaS hero block, no 2-column video-left/grid-right layout, no rounded bubbly cards, and no green neon rings.

## Page Arc

### Page: Home (The Acoustic Observatory)
- **Page-Role Scene:** Soundstage Ingest & Harmonic Decryption Deck.
- **Page Scene Thesis:** The user is not using an ordinary web app; they are operating a specialized acoustic decoding terminal where songs are broken down into their elemental harmonic frequencies.
- **One Big Idea:** The Widescreen Glass Chamber—where the video/audio stream and the emerging active chord interact as a single monumental specimen.
- **Hero Dominance Statement:** The hero commands total authority through an anamorphic letterbox stage with corner brackets, coordinate telemetry, and a monolithic mode switch (YouTube Stream vs. Local Wasm Engine).
- **Restraint Statement:** Color is stripped down to raw earth-and-slate tones with zero gradients. Color appears only as functional state (amber phosphor signal and muted acoustic lock).
- **Material Thesis:** Heavy acoustic dampening panels, anti-reflective glass, and cathode display readouts.
- **Typography Thesis:** Sharp, condensed technical coordinates contrasting with colossal, heavy chord roots.
- **Narrative Arc:**
  1. *Approach & Contact (Scene 1: Ingest Deck)*: Establish coordinates, input audio stream or drop file.
  2. *The Chamber (Scene 2: Active Decryption)*: Simultaneous video/audio observation and massive root-note manifestation.
  3. *Harmonic Spectrogram (Scene 3: Continuous Timeline Tape)*: A linear, horizontal tape of chord segments advancing like seismic readings.
  4. *Calibration (Scene 4: Harmonic Matrix Controls)*: Fine-tuning transpositions, capo positioning, and millisecond sync alignment.
- **Hero Archetype:** `#4 Letterbox Cinema`
- **Signature Composition:** Symmetrical Center-Weighted Monument (`#1`) with 70/30 Dominant Split (`#35`) anchored by Horizontal Seismic Scroll (`#24`).
- **Grid Fallback Test:** If converted to a generic grid, the continuous acoustic timeline and monolithic observation chamber would disintegrate into fragmented, incoherent cards.
- **Shared System Holdback:** Top bar and bottom status dock remain ultra-thin, never intruding on the focal specimen chamber.
- **UI Exposure Guardrail:** All director/film references remain strictly internal to the architecture; the interface is branded purely as ChordSense AI.
